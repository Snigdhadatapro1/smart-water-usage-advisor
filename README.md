# 💧 AI-Based Smart Water Usage Advisor

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI-Based Smart Water Usage Advisor is a Python-based decision-support prototype designed to promote responsible and sustainable water consumption.

The system analyzes user-provided information such as household size, estimated daily water usage, water-using activities, and water-related concerns. It then provides personalized water-saving recommendations.

The project is aligned with **SDG 6 – Clean Water and Sanitation**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Water is an essential natural resource, but unnecessary water consumption can occur through inefficient daily activities, excessive usage, leakage, and lack of awareness.

Many households and individuals do not have a simple way to understand their water usage or receive personalized guidance for reducing unnecessary consumption.

This project aims to provide an accessible digital advisor that analyzes basic water-use information and suggests practical water-saving actions.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The Smart Water Usage Advisor provides:

- Household water usage analysis
- Estimated water usage per person
- Water usage categorization
- Personalized water-saving recommendations
- Information retrieval from a water-conservation knowledge base
- Interactive user interface using Gradio
- Water usage visualizations
- Responsible AI considerations

---

## 🤖 AI Approach

The prototype uses:

- **TF-IDF-based information retrieval**
- **Cosine similarity**
- **Knowledge-base retrieval**
- **Rule-based personalized recommendations**
- **Python-based decision-support logic**

The retrieval component identifies relevant water-conservation information based on the user's query or concerns.
